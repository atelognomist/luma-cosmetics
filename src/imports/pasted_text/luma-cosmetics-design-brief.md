MASTER FIGMA MAKE PROMPT — LUMA COSMETICS
0. ROLE

Act as a senior product designer, e-commerce UX designer, conversion-rate optimization specialist, brand designer, and art director working together.

Design a complete, production-quality cosmetics e-commerce website for a modern Algerian beauty business.

The website must feel like a real, established cosmetics brand, not an AI-generated website template, startup landing page, SaaS dashboard, or generic Shopify theme.

The temporary brand name is:

LUMA

Treat LUMA as a temporary working name throughout the design so the brand name can easily be replaced later.

The website should be designed as a real commercial product, with a scalable design system and complete page architecture.

1. BUSINESS CONCEPT

LUMA is a modern cosmetics and beauty products store targeting women and girls of all ages, primarily in Algeria.

The store can eventually contain hundreds of products across major beauty categories.

The website's main purpose is:

Show products beautifully → make customers want them → help them discover the right products → build trust → allow them to contact the business and order.

This is not initially a traditional full online checkout platform.

The primary conversion path is:

Browse products → Product page → Add to selection → Continue browsing → Review selection → Contact us → Confirm order and delivery

Customers should be able to contact the business through:

WhatsApp
Instagram
Messenger

The website should be architected so that delivery-company APIs can be integrated later.

For now, delivery information should be presented clearly, with exact delivery price/time confirmed during the order process.

2. CORE BRAND POSITIONING

The main brand identity should be:

Personalized Beauty Boutique

However, the website should contain different sections that communicate different aspects of the brand:

Premium Beauty

Sophisticated presentation, beautiful photography, high-quality visual hierarchy.

Affordable Beauty

Accessible prices, visible DZD pricing, genuine offers and good value.

Trendy Beauty

Modern products, current trends, campaign-driven merchandising and social-media-friendly presentation.

Personalized Boutique

The feeling that the customer is discovering products that fit her preferences rather than browsing an anonymous warehouse.

Do NOT make the website feel cheap just because affordability is part of the positioning.
